    Immortal Jellyfish

    Turritopsis dohrnii, also known as the Immortal Jellyfish, is a creature with the ability to defy its own death. It does this by turning itself back into a polyp whenever it is damaged or aging. The way this works is through a biological mechanism called transdifferation where the adult cells are able to un-differentiate and transform into different cells without DNA degradation.
